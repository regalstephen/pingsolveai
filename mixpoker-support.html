Common Questions

Is Mix Poker real-money gambling?

No. Every table and tournament uses practice chips. They have no cash value, can't be bought or cashed out, and there is no real-money gambling. You start with 20,000 chips and get a daily bonus.

What do I get for free?

A free Mix Poker account gets the THORSE equity calculators, a 20,000-chip practice stack and the Beginner band of the ladder: Dottie, Pip and Gus. Free accounts get a 1,000-chip daily bonus and one free seat a day if they bust.

How does the ladder work?

Fourteen opponents in four bands: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Master. Beat one in a rated heads-up match and the next unlocks. You earn a rating in every game and in the mix. More about the Practice Tables.

What happens if I bust out of a tournament?

Re-enter the same event from level 1 whenever you are ready. Need to stop mid-event? Leave the table: your seat is saved, the tournament pauses, and it is waiting on any of your devices when you come back. More about the MTT Circuit.

Can I play on the web?

Yes. Mix Poker also runs in the browser at learnmixpoker.com.
