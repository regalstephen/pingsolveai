Questions

Good to know.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so we quote after the free call. You’ll have a clear price before any work starts. Coaching is paid remote sessions, and we’ll tell you the price on the call.

How long does it take?

Small automations and setups are quicker than full apps. Your plan includes a timeline, so you know what to expect before you commit.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Tell us the problem in your own words. If you choose coaching, we go at your pace and explain everything in plain English.

Is everything remote?

Yes. Calls, demos and coaching all happen over video and screen sharing, wherever you are.

Which AI do you use?

Whatever fits the job: Claude, ChatGPT, or models that run on your own computer. We’ll explain the trade-offs so you can choose.

What happens to my data?

We set things up in your accounts, connect only what the job needs, and explain where your data goes. With a private AI setup, your conversations can stay on your own computer.

What happens after it’s built?

It’s yours. We hand everything over, show you how it works, and we’re a message away when you want to change something.
