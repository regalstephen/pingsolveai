The rules

The same rules are in the app: tap the ? on Home.

🔦

Safari

  1. Point the phone at the floor and sweep it slowly until the room is mapped.
  2. You have 3 minutes. Walk slowly and look all around: on the floor, and up in the air too.
  3. When you spot a sleeping beast, creep closer, put it in the middle of the frame and hold steady.
  4. Tap the shutter when it says “Now!” and hold still for a moment. Every photo scores points.
  5. A ? above a beast means it is stirring, and ! means it is waking up. Freeze until it settles.
  6. Noise and fast moves fill Ranger’s Nerve. If it fills up, every beast runs away and the round ends.
  7. A loud noise or a sudden jolt wakes every beast close by, even ones you haven’t found yet. They tiptoe away and still count as spooked.
  8. Closed the app and opened it somewhere else? Go back to where you were, or tap Map from here and sweep the floor again.
📖

Sticker Book

  1. The first photo of each kind of beast becomes a sticker in your Sticker Book, and is worth ×2 points.
  2. Rarer beasts are worth more: Common 10 · Uncommon 25 · Rare 60 · Legendary 150 points.
  3. Your first photo of the day is worth double. Come back each night to keep your streak going.
  4. Each night one free beast is Tonight’s Sleeper (it’s on Home). Its photo is worth double too.
  5. The 5 gold slots are Golden Stickers: the rarest sleepers, part of Complete the Sticker Book.
  6. Once a day a golden visitor may sneak into a Safari. Snap it for the points; its sticker stays locked until you complete the Sticker Book.
  7. After you complete the Sticker Book, some beasts turn up shiny: solid gold, with sparkles all around. A shiny photo is stamped SHINY, is worth double, and puts a ✨ mark on its sticker.
🎖️

Your Ranger

  1. On the Ranger tab you can change your name and pick a buddy beast. Your buddy waits for you on Home.
  2. Missions earn 8 Ranger Badges. Tap a medal on the Ranger tab to see what it is for.
  3. Every photo you take lands in your Beast Album, on the Ranger tab. Share it or save it to your phone.
  4. Complete the Sticker Book adds Golden Buddies, and Decorate in the Album: crowns, hats and props for your photos.
🎉

Party Safari

With Complete the Sticker Book

  1. 2 to 8 players, one phone. Type everyone’s names in turn order.
  2. Each player gets 75 seconds of Safari, then passes the phone on.
  3. Tap “I’m [name] — go!” only when it is really your turn.
  4. Most points at the end wins. Ties share the medal.
🧸

Ranger Training

Free

  1. 4 heists that teach you to move quietly. All free.
  2. Map the floor, then tap the ground to put the sleeping beast down.
  3. Creep to the treasure without waking it: keep quiet, move smoothly, and never step on the beast.
  4. Get close to the loot and grab it. On later heists the beast wakes up groggy when you do: back away slowly and get clear within 15 seconds.
  5. Each heist has three goals: 💎 take the loot, 📸 a close, steady Trophy photo, and 👻 win without it ever getting close to waking.
  6. Complete the Sticker Book adds Hardcore mode here: twitchier beasts and tighter clocks.
🏴‍☠️

Party Heist

With Complete the Sticker Book

  1. Everyone plays the same heist, one turn each, passing the phone.
  2. Points: 100 for each piece of loot, 150 for getting away, plus a calm bonus for keeping the beast sleepy.
  3. Wake the beast and you keep only the loot you had grabbed. Biggest haul wins.
★

Ranger tips

  • Play in a bright room with clear floor space. Plain, dark or shiny floors are hard for the camera to see.
  • Move like a ninja: slow steps, steady hands, quiet voices.
  • Look around you, not just ahead. Beasts sleep behind you and up high too.
  • Ask a grown-up to help clear anything you could trip over.

Questions people ask

On a safari

A beast got spooked that I never even saw. Why?

A loud noise or a sudden jolt wakes every sleeper close by, even ones you haven’t found yet. They tiptoe away before you can see them, but they still count as spooked. Stay quiet and the room stays full.

What do the ? and ! above a beast mean?

That beast is stirring. A ? means it’s starting to wake; a ! means it’s nearly awake. Freeze, stay quiet, and it settles back down.

Rushing up to a beast or talking near it wakes it, and rarer beasts wake faster. Creep in slowly.

What is Ranger’s Nerve?

The meter that fills when you’re noisy or move fast. If it fills right up, every beast runs away and the round ends. Slow down and keep quiet and it stays low.

I closed the app and now the beasts are gone. What’s “Map from here”?

The phone remembers the room by what it saw. If you leave the app and come back somewhere else, the game says it lost track of the room. Go back to where you were, or tap Map from here and sweep the floor again.

What is Tonight’s Sleeper?

Each night one free beast is Tonight’s Sleeper. Home shows which one. Its photo is worth double, and that stacks with First Catch of the Day. It works in Party Safari too.

What is First Catch of the Day?

Your first photo each day is worth double, and the game counts how many days in a row you’ve caught a beast. Come back tomorrow to keep the streak going.

I saw a golden beast, but its sticker is locked. Why?

That was the golden visitor. Once a day, one of the five Golden Sticker beasts may sneak into a solo Safari. Snap it and you keep the points; its sticker stays locked until you complete the Sticker Book. Your photo is kept, so buying later fills that sticker in.

What are shiny beasts?

After you complete the Sticker Book, about one beast in twenty on a solo Safari turns up shiny: solid gold, with sparkles all around. A shiny photo is stamped SHINY, is worth double and puts a ✨ on that sticker. Shiny photos you save or share leave in a golden frame.

How are photos scored?

Common 10, Uncommon 25, Rare 60 and Legendary 150 points. A beast you’ve never photographed before is worth ×2, and so are your first catch of the day and Tonight’s Sleeper.

Your ranger

What are the Ranger Badges?

Eight medals on the Ranger tab, each for a mission:

  • First Sticker: stick your first photo in the Sticker Book.
  • Sky Watcher: photograph flying beasts.
  • Full House: collect every free sticker.
  • Whisper Feet: finish a Safari with a photo and nothing spooked.
  • Big Haul: score big in one Safari.
  • Seven Nights: catch a beast seven nights in a row.
  • Party Host: finish a Party Safari or Party Heist.
  • Graduate: clear all four Ranger Training heists.

Tap a medal in the app to see exactly what it needs. Complete the Sticker Book also adds a Golden Ranger badge.

How do I change my name or buddy beast?

On the Ranger tab. Your buddy waits for you on Home. Complete the Sticker Book adds the five Golden Buddies.

Where are my photos?

In the Beast Album on the Ranger tab. They stay inside the app on your phone until you tap Save (to your photo library) or Share. With Complete the Sticker Book, tap a photo and then Decorate to add crowns, hats and props.

Party Mode

How does Party Mode work?

2 to 8 players share one phone, taking turns and passing it on. There are two games:

  • Party Safari: each player gets 75 seconds of Safari. Most points wins; ties share the medal.
  • Party Heist: everyone plays the same heist, one turn each. Biggest haul wins.

Party Mode comes with Complete the Sticker Book.

How is a Party Heist scored?
  • 100 for each piece of loot you take. You keep it even if the beast wakes.
  • +150 for getting away.
  • A calm bonus of up to 100 when you get away: the sleepier the beast stayed, the bigger it is.

Free and paid

Is it really free?

Yes. Every solo Beast Safari and all four Ranger Training heists are free, not a trial. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no energy timers.

What does Complete the Sticker Book add?

It’s the one optional purchase, US$1.99 once (Apple shows your own local price before anything is charged). It adds:

  • the five rare and legendary Golden Stickers
  • shiny beasts: solid gold, sparkling and worth double
  • Party Mode: Party Safari and Party Heist
  • Golden Ranger extras: photo props, Golden Buddies, a golden frame on shiny photos and the Golden Ranger badge
  • Hardcore mode in Ranger Training
What is the grown-up check?

Before the price button buys anything, the app asks a grown-up to hold the button for two seconds or answer a sum. Restore Purchase needs no check.

I paid, but it’s locked on my new phone. How do I restore?

Tap Restore Purchase: it’s under Ranger › About & Help, and on the Complete the Sticker Book screen. As long as you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with, it unlocks straight away and you’re not charged again.

If nothing is found, check the Apple Account under Settings is the one you bought with. That’s by far the most common cause.

I bought the full game when it was Sleeping Beasts. Do I pay again?

No. It’s the same app with a new name, and the same purchase. You already have everything Complete the Sticker Book adds. If it shows as locked, tap Restore Purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us; we have no way to issue one. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase. If something is broken, please email us too so we can fix it.

Privacy and devices

Are you recording my voice or my room?

No. The microphone measures how loud the room is: a single number, read on your phone and thrown away straight away. No audio is recorded, stored or sent, and there’s no speech recognition of any kind. It’s only on during a Safari round or a heist.

The camera draws the room on screen and keeps nothing. The only pictures are the ones you take with the shutter or photo button, and they stay in the app on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhone XS and newer. The game uses Apple’s ARKit to find real surfaces, which needs that hardware. You don’t need a Pro model or LiDAR. It’s built for phones, so it isn’t offered on iPad.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to buy or restore. The game itself runs entirely on your phone, so you can play with the Wi-Fi off.

Is it scary? My child is little.

No. On a safari, a beast that wakes just slips away. In Ranger Training the worst that happens is a plush toy sitting up and shouting “INTRUDER ALERT!” No gore, no chasing, no monsters coming for you. It’s rated 4+.

Wasn’t this called Sleeping Beasts?

Yes. Version 1.1 renamed Sleeping Beasts: AR Stealth to Beast Safari: AR Photo Hunt, because the photo safari became the heart of the game. The stealth heists are still here, as Ranger Training.

Fixes

The game can’t find my floor.

Nine times out of ten it’s light. Turn on a lamp, then point the camera at a floor with some pattern or texture: a rug, floorboards, a carpet weave. Plain, very dark or shiny floors give the camera nothing to lock onto. Move the phone slowly; sweeping fast makes it worse.

Still stuck after a few seconds? On a safari a Start anyway button appears: flying beasts come first, and floor beasts join as the floor gets mapped. In Ranger Training a Put it down here button drops the beast in front of you without a scan.

I can’t find any beasts.

Turn all the way around and look up as well as down: beasts sleep behind you and near the ceiling. Walk to another part of the room, and sweep the floor around you so more sleepers can settle in. If the game says it lost track of the room, go back to where you were or tap Map from here.

The beasts wake up the moment I start.

The room is probably loud. A TV, a dishwasher or a busy kitchen fills the meters before you take a step. Find a quieter room, and move at about half your normal walking speed. The first heist is the most forgiving place to practise.

I said no to the camera or microphone.

Open the iPhone’s Settings, scroll down to Beast Safari, and turn on Camera and Microphone. The game can’t show the beasts without the camera, or hear you without the microphone.

Do I need a big room? Can I play sitting down?

Ranger Training needs about a metre of clear floor; a living-room rug is plenty. A safari is happier in a whole room you can walk around. You can play sitting on the floor and leaning, but it’s much more fun on your feet.

For grown-ups

Four things worth two minutes of your time, the first one most of all.

⚠️ Safety comes first

This game has people walking around their own home while looking at a screen. Please:

  • Clear the floor first: cables, toys, low tables, pets.
  • Never play near stairs, balconies, glass doors, water or a hot stove.
  • Never play outdoors near traffic or in a car park.
  • Supervise younger players the whole time, and keep other children and pets out of the play area.
  • Look up often: the camera view won’t warn you about the coffee table.

Full safety and health guidance is in the Terms of Service.

🎙️ About that microphone

The beasts “hear” you, so the game needs microphone access. Here’s exactly what that means:

  • It reads one number: how loud the room is. That’s all.
  • There is no recording, ever. No audio file is created, saved or sent anywhere.
  • It’s only on during a Safari round or a heist, and switches off the moment you leave.
  • No speech recognition. No listening for words. Nothing leaves the phone.

The camera works the same way: it draws the room on screen and keeps nothing. Details in the Privacy Policy.
