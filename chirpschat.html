The daily loop

Five minutes, one good argument

Every day there’s one two-sided question, taken from the news and checked so both sides get a fair shot. Here’s how a day goes.

  1. Pick a side

    Vote before you see anything else. The split stays hidden until you do.

  2. Read the room

    Guess what share of players will pick your side. The closer you are, the more Clout you earn.

  3. Make your case

    Post one chirp of up to 320 characters. You only get one a day, so make it count.

  4. Judge the takes

    Rate three takes from both sides, or six if you’re on a roll.

  5. Switch once

    If a take changes your mind, switch and credit the player who convinced you.

  6. Come back for results

    The final split, your score and your chirp’s rank land when the next question drops.

Make your case

Vote on the argument, not the side

Once you’ve played, you can read every chirp on today’s question and spend 5 votes on the strongest and weakest arguments. They stay hidden until then, so nobody is swayed before deciding.

  • The other side counts double. A “Good point” from someone who disagrees with you is worth more.
  • Change a mind, get the credit. When a player switches because of your chirp, you earn Clout for it.
  • Strong takes from the other side come first, so you hear the best case against you.

Clout

Earn it by being right, and by being convincing

Clout comes from playing every day, reading the room well and changing minds. It can’t be bought. Your tier ring shows next to every chirp you post.

Fair play

One of each, every day

One vote, one switch, one chirp and 5 chirp votes per player per day. You can’t vote on your own chirp.

Real players come first

When a question is new, some takes are AI starter takes, clearly labeled. They drop out as player chirps come in and never count toward results.

Reports, not pile-ons

Downvotes never hide a chirp; reports do. And Clout can’t be bought.
