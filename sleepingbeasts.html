Free to play · iPhone · AR

Something is sleeping in your house

Beast Safari turns your real room into a photo safari. Sleepy plush beasts curl up on your floor and drift near your ceiling. Find them, creep close and snap their picture — quietly.

Get it on the App StoreFree · iPhone XS or newer

No ads. No accounts. No chat.

Sneak. Snap. Collect.

Point your phone around the room and the beasts appear in true 360° augmented reality, right where you are.

🤫

Sneak

Walk slowly and keep it down. The game listens to how loud your room is, and noise makes the beasts stir.

📸

Snap

Creep close, put a beast in the middle of the frame, hold steady and tap the shutter. Small hands welcome: a little wobble won’t ruin the shot.

📖

Collect

Your first photo of each beast becomes a sticker. Every photo lands in your Beast Album, ready to share.

Fill your Sticker Book

Made for the whole house

No blood, no gore, no horror. The loudest thing in the game is a startled plushie, and being terrible at it is most of the fun.

No ads, no accounts, no chat

No sign-up, no strangers, no subscriptions, no energy timers.

The microphone never records

The game reads one number, how loud the room is, right on your phone. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Photos stay on your phone

They live in the app’s own Beast Album, and reach your photo library only when you tap Save. Nothing is uploaded.

A grown-up check

Before any purchase: hold the button for two seconds or answer a sum. Restoring a purchase needs no check.

Please clear the floor before you play and keep an eye on younger rangers. Safety tips for grown-ups

What you need

  • An iPhone XS or newer (ARKit is required; no Pro model or LiDAR needed)
  • A bit of clear floor, roughly the size of a rug
  • A room lit well enough for the camera to find the floor
  • Somewhere you’re allowed to be quiet
