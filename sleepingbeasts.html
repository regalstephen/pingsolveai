Something is asleep on your floor.

Sleeping Beasts turns your actual room into a stealth course. Point your phone at the ground and a snoring plush monster settles onto your real carpet. Now creep past it, take the treasure, and whatever you do — don't wake it up.

How It Works

If you can walk quietly, you already know how to play. Turn your living room, hallway, or bedroom into a live AR stealth challenge.

1. Pick Your Room

Clear a small patch of floor about the size of a bath towel. Turn on a light and push shoes or cables out of the way.

2. Scan the Ground

Point your iPhone camera down and sweep slowly side to side. In seconds, ARKit detects your room's surface.

3. Put a Beast to Bed

Tap the floor! A sleeping monster flops down right on your carpet. Walk around it — it's right there in the room with you.

4. Creep & Take Treasure

Walk slowly, hold your phone steady, and stay quiet. Grab the goal and back out before it wakes up!

What Wakes Them Up

Watch the three meters on your screen and keep them in the safe zone.

🔊 Noise

The app measures room loudness. A quiet whisper is okay, but a dropped shoe or loud laughter will wake the beast instantly!

〰️ Wobble

Stomping or swinging your phone makes the beast twitch. Smooth, slow movements keep it snoring peacefully.

💤 Snooze Cone

Monsters dream facing forward. Lingering directly in front of a beast's nose for too long starts to stir it up.
