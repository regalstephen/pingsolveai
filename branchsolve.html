Product

There is no builder. That is the product.

Every other tool in this category hands you a canvas and a weekend. Branchsolve asks you to describe the process, then does the rest — and shows its work before anything goes live.

  1. 01

    Describe it

    Tell your assistant how approvals work today, in your own words. Thresholds, who signs off, what happens when nobody responds.

  2. 02

    See where requests land

    Before anything is live, the simulator derives the boundary cases from your own rules and shows you exactly who gets each one.

  3. 03

    Publish

    One confirmation. The workflow is live, versioned, and your team can submit against it immediately.

  4. 04

    Approve in chat

    Requests reach approvers where they already work. They decide without opening another tool, and the record lands in Branchsolve.

Know where requests land before the first one is submitted

Branchsolve reads the workflow’s own conditions and works out the cases that sit either side of every threshold. It also warns you when a workflow would let something through with nobody approving it.

  • The spec, not the code

    Your assistant turns a description into a declarative workflow spec — fields, steps, approvers, conditions. It is data, never a program, so nothing generated is ever executed.

  • An audit trail nobody can edit

    Decisions are append-only. Not even an administrator can change one. A correction is a new decision with its own actor and timestamp.

  • Versions that hold still

    A published workflow is immutable. Change it and in-flight requests finish on the version they started on.
