How it works

Talk the way you would to a friend

Your go-between helps you turn what’s going on into a short note in your own words, and passes it on only when you say so.

  1. Tell your go-between

    Chat with Pal or Birdie, or call and talk out loud. Say what’s new, big or small.

  2. Shape it into a note

    When there’s something worth passing on, it helps you write a short note in your words and reads it back.

  3. Send it when you say so

    To a whole circle, or to one friend. Nothing leaves your chat until you say yes.

Hear from your friends

Their news, read to you word for word

When friends send you notes, your go-between reads them to you exactly as they wrote them, in chat or on a call. Then you can write back.

  • No feed to scroll. Just the people you chose, when they have something to say.
  • Circles or one friend. Share with a group, or keep it between two of you.
  • Out loud or in chat. Call your go-between on a walk, or type when it’s quiet.
