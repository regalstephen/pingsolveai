Your beat. Your battle.

Play a rhythm level. Make your own. Share the code and race a friend's recorded run.

Create, clear, share

Record taps into a note pattern, fine-tune it in the grid, and use LOOP to copy and paste sections. Clear your level to verify it before publishing. Remix a community level or learn with the authored Climb.

Hitline records note patterns, not your song audio. Song credits help players find music separately.

Your existing account and Pro purchase

Hitline uses the existing TouchBattle app listing and supported premium product. Existing owners can restore Pro with the Apple account that purchased it. Drafts and Climb progress stay on the device.
