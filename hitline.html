Learn it in seconds

Five note types. One finger.

Notes fall toward the hit line. Hit them on the beat to build your combo. Perfects build it fastest.

  • Tap

    Tap the lane when a circle reaches the line.

  • Hold

    Hold the bar until it ends.

  • Slide

    Press the first circle, then follow the line to the second.

  • Swipe

    Slice through the diamond just before it reaches the line.

  • Red

    Never touch red.

Create

Record it like you play it

Tap pads along to your music and your timing becomes a level. Then fine-tune it in the grid.

  • Record, then edit. Tap to the beat, then move, add or change any note in the grid.
  • LOOP to build fast. Copy and paste whole bars so a level comes together quickly.
  • Clear it to publish. Play your own level to verify it before anyone else can.
  • Your music stays yours. Hitline saves note patterns, not song audio. Add an optional artist and song credit so players can find the track.

Share & race

Send a code. Race the run.

Every published level has a short code. Send it to a friend, then race their recorded run on the leaderboard.

  • Remix any community level and make it your own.
  • Like the levels you love so they rise up Popular.
  • A Featured level every day, plus New, Popular and Hardest lists.
  • Go 4 lanes with chords, slides and hazards when you want more.

Questions

More on the help page, or email support@pingsolveai.com.

What happened to TouchBattle?

Hitline is the new version of the TouchBattle app. Update the app you already have; it’s the same App Store listing, now called Hitline: TouchBattle.

Does Hitline play my music?

No. Hitline saves the note pattern you made, not the song. Add an optional artist and song credit so players can play the music on their own.

Who can see my levels?

Levels you publish and your leaderboard runs are visible to other players. Unlisted levels can be opened by anyone who has their code. You can report a level or block a creator in the app.

Are my drafts backed up?

Drafts and Climb progress stay on your device, and signing in doesn’t back them up. Keep the app installed to keep them; don’t delete it to troubleshoot.
