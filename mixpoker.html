Six Games, One Rotation

THORSE is six of the most popular poker variants played in a single rotation. Instead of specializing in one game, you adapt every orbit, across draw games, flop games and stud. Play one game to drill it, or a HORSE, TORSE or full THORSE rotation.

Practice Tables & the Ladder

Fourteen opponents, four bands, every one with its own style. Beat one in a rated heads-up match and the next unlocks, from Beginner to Master. Heads-up or 6-max, all six games, and every decision graded after the match. The Beginner ladder is free.

  • A rating for every game plus a mixed rating, Elo style
  • Stakes rise with the band: 5/10 at Beginner up to 50/100 at Master
  • Rated or casual matches of 30, 60 or 90 hands

The MTT Circuit

Ten multi-table tournaments, ten bracelets. Start in a 12-player opener and work up to the 120-player World Mix Game Championship. Limits rise, tables break, the field thins, the top 15% cash, and the last player standing takes the bracelet.

Win an event to unlock the next. Bust out? Re-enter from level 1. Leave any time: your seat waits.
