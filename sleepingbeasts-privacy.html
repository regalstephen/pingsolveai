The short version. Beast Safari is built to need as little of your information as possible. Your microphone, your camera, and your motion sensors are read only on your own device, only while you are playing, and the data from them is never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted to us or anyone else. The photos you take and the names you type stay on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no email collection, no advertising, and no tracking. The only information that leaves your device is an anonymous purchase receipt and, if the App crashes, a technical crash report.

1. Information Processed Entirely On Your Device (Not Collected)

The following are fundamental to how the App works. They are processed in real time on your device and discarded immediately. They are never recorded to storage, never transmitted off your device, and never received by PingSolve or by any third party.

A. Microphone Audio

The core mechanic of the App is that a sleeping creature can “hear” you. To make that work, the App measures the ambient loudness level (decibels) of your environment while a game session is actively running.

  • The App reads a numeric volume level only. It does not perform speech recognition, voice identification, transcription, or content analysis of any kind.
  • No audio is ever recorded, buffered to disk, saved, retained, or transmitted. There is no audio file at any point.
  • The microphone is activated only during active gameplay and is released as soon as you leave that screen.
  • You may deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings. The App will continue to run.

B. Camera Imagery

The App uses augmented reality, which requires the camera to detect flat surfaces such as your floor so that a virtual creature can be drawn as if it were in your room.

  • Camera frames are used solely to render the live augmented reality view on your screen and to allow Apple’s ARKit to detect surfaces.
  • No photographs, video, or camera frames are recorded, saved, or transmitted except a still image you deliberately capture yourself (see Section 2).
  • We do not perform facial recognition, biometric analysis, object identification, room mapping for retention, or any other analysis of your surroundings.

C. Motion & Device Sensors

Accelerometer and device-motion readings are used to detect sudden movement during gameplay. These values are evaluated in the moment and discarded. They are never recorded or transmitted.

2. Photographs and Names Kept on Your Device

The App includes an optional photo feature that lets you capture an image of a virtual creature in your environment.

  • A photograph is created only when you deliberately tap the capture control.
  • Photographs are kept in the App’s own Beast Album, in the App’s private storage on your device. A photograph is copied to your own device’s photo library only when you choose to save it there.
  • Decorating a photograph with props creates a new copy on your device; the original is not changed. You can delete photographs from the Beast Album at any time.
  • PingSolve never receives, uploads, accesses, stores, or transmits your photographs. We have no server that could receive them.
  • If you choose to share a photograph using your device’s share sheet, that transmission is governed by the privacy policy of whatever application or service you share it to. We are not a party to it.

The App also lets you type a ranger name and, in Party Mode, the names of the players taking turns. These names are stored only on your device, used only to show them in the game, and never transmitted to us or anyone else. We suggest first names or nicknames.

Everything in this Section is deleted when you delete the App from your device, except photographs you have saved to your photo library or shared.

3. Information That Does Leave Your Device

A. Purchase Information

The App offers a single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchase processing is handled by Apple, and entitlement management is handled by our service provider RevenueCat, Inc.

  • We receive an anonymous application user identifier generated by RevenueCat and a record of whether the purchase entitlement is active.
  • This identifier is not linked to your name, email address, Apple Account, or any real-world identity. The App never calls any log-in or identification function, and there are no user accounts.
  • We never receive your payment card number, billing address, or financial details. Those are handled entirely by Apple and are never disclosed to us.

B. Crash & Diagnostic Data

If the App crashes or encounters a serious error, a technical diagnostic report may be sent to our service provider Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) so that we can find and fix the defect.

  • A report may include the device model, operating system version, App version, the technical stack trace of the failure, and non-personal breadcrumbs describing what part of the game was running.
  • Crash reports never include audio, camera imagery, photographs, or the contents of your environment. Screenshot attachment is explicitly disabled in our configuration precisely because the camera view would show your home.
  • Crash data is used solely to diagnose and repair defects. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking.
