Frequently asked questions

All questions
How much does it cost to post a listing on ChairGrab?

Posting costs a one-time $5 fee per listing. There is no subscription and no monthly charge. Browsing, searching, and contacting listers is always free.

Is it free to find a chair or a salon job?

Yes. Searching listings, browsing city pages, using our AI assistant, and contacting salon owners are all free. Each listing shows the owner’s contact details.

Do I need an account to post?

No. You can post without an account, but signing up first lets you edit, hide, or delete your listing later from your profile.
